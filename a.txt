**1. Introduction**

One-liner:

"An AI-powered workspace that transforms scattered requirements, interviews, and stakeholder conversations into a traceable, structured knowledge graph for Business Analysts."

Tagline:

BA Workspace is a next-generation platform designed for Business Analysts who need to manage requirements, stakeholder feedback, process models, and documentation across complex projects. Powered by coordinated AI agents, it unifies documents, conversations, and decisions into a living system that ensures traceability, consistency, and clarity.

**2. User Persona**

Alex — Senior Business Analyst

Profile:

- Position: Senior Business Analyst
- Location: Da Nang, Vietnam
- Age: 32
- Industry: Digital transformation & software implementation

Goals:

- Maintain clear traceability from stakeholder needs → requirements → deliverables
- Reduce time spent searching for documents, notes, and decisions
- Ensure requirement consistency across multiple project phases
- Quickly validate deliverables against business rules and stakeholder expectations
- Collaborate effectively with distributed teams

Frustrations:

- Requirements scattered across Word docs, PDFs, Slack threads, and interview notes
- No unified view of stakeholder feedback and how it impacts requirements
- Constant rework due to missed dependencies or conflicting requirements
- Manual cross-checking of requirements against business rules
- Difficulty maintaining Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM) as projects evolve

Problem Statement:

Alex manages multiple digital transformation projects where requirements come from diverse stakeholders through interviews, workshops, emails, and meetings. Critical information gets buried in dozens of documents, making it difficult to validate consistency, trace decisions, and ensure nothing falls through the cracks. Alex needs a workspace where all BA artifacts are connected, AI helps validate deliverables, and traceability is automatic — not manual.

3. Process Agent (Jack)

4. Compliance Agent (David)

5. Traceability Agent (Paul)

6. Orchestration Agent (CORE)

**6. How It Works**

👤 Business Analyst (Alex) → Validate Requirements Document

User Action: Alex uploads a requirements document and clicks "Validate"

AI Response:

- Emma (Requirements Agent) checks each requirement for consistency, clarity, completeness
- Sarah (Stakeholder Agent) maps requirements to stakeholder needs
- David (Compliance Agent) verifies BABOK compliance
- Paul (Traceability Agent) identifies missing links
- CORE produces unified validation report with:
    - Consistency issues (conflicts, duplicates)
    - Quality scores per requirement
    - Stakeholder alignment gaps
    - Compliance checklist
    - Recommended actions

Outcome: Alex gets a comprehensive validation report in seconds, catching issues before stakeholder review.

📊 Business Analyst (Alex) → Analyze Stakeholder Feedback

User Action: Alex uploads 5 stakeholder interview transcripts and asks "What are the key concerns?"

AI Response:

- Sarah (Stakeholder Agent) analyzes all interviews
- Identifies common themes and concerns
- Maps stakeholder positions and conflicts
- Highlights high-priority needs
- Links feedback to existing requirements
- Suggests new requirements based on unmet needs

Outcome: Alex sees a stakeholder alignment dashboard showing consensus areas, conflicts, and gaps — with evidence linked to source interviews.

🔄 Business Analyst (Alex) → Update Process Flow

User Action: Alex modifies a BPMN process diagram and asks "What requirements are impacted?"

AI Response:

- Jack (Process Agent) validates the updated process flow
- Paul (Traceability Agent) identifies linked requirements
- CORE generates impact analysis showing:
    - Requirements that need review
    - Stakeholders to notify
    - Deliverables that may change
    - Risk assessment

Outcome: Alex knows exactly what to update and who to consult before propagating the change.

**10. Goals & Non-Goals**

✅ Goals

1. Clear navigation — Find any BA artifact in ≤3 clicks
2. Automatic traceability — RTM generates itself, no manual linking
3. Deliverable validation — AI catches defects before human review
4. Knowledge reuse — Patterns and templates learned from past projects
5. Collaboration clarity — Everyone sees the same truth, no version confusion

❌ Non-Goals (MVP)

1. Full project management (no Gantt, EVM, resource allocation)
2. Integration with all enterprise systems (focus on document import/export)
3. Real-time co-editing (asynchronous collaboration is sufficient)
4. Custom AI agent creation (predefined BA agents only)
5. Advanced BPMN simulation (validation only, not execution)
